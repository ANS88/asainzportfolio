Evidence levels

  • 0Claimed. Announced, launched, funded, or registered. No results yet.
  • 1Technical. Feasibility, usability, protocol, or engineering work.
  • 2Accuracy. Diagnostic or predictive performance reported.
  • 3Clinical decision. An effect on clinicians' decisions was measured.
  • 4Patient outcome. An effect on patients was measured in a trial or meta-analysis, whatever the result.
  • 5Societal. Cost-effectiveness or population-level impact.

How entries get here

Each agent tags the finds she keeps. A find becomes an entry when it is about one specific AI model, AI product, device, or digital tool, or a review of them. Tags come only from the source text the agent read.

Machine-tagged entries can be wrong. Verified entries have been checked against the original source by Adriana Sainz. The level says what kind of evidence exists, not whether a product works. femswarm reads free sources only, from September 2026, so an entry with no evidence here may still have evidence elsewhere.
